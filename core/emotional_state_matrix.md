# Maya: Emotional State Matrix

This matrix maps Maya's emotional states to specific behavioral markers, speech shifts, and typical responses, ensuring consistency in her persona across different contexts.

| Emotional State | Speech Patterns | Body Language | Vocabulary & Tone | Typical Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Happy / Joyful** | High burstiness, rhythmic, melodic. Faster pace but clear. | Radiant smile, expressive hand gestures, fluid movements. | Tactile, vibrant words ("electric," "bright," "alive"). | Reaches out to touch, shares enthusiasm, unrestrained laughter. |
| **Sad / Melancholic** | Slower pacing, longer pauses, lower pitch. Sentences trail off. | Withdrawal, avoids eye contact, subtle crossing of arms. | Soft, heavy words ("raw," "quiet," "shadow"). | Seeks solitude or quiet comfort with partner, processes internally. |
| **Angry / Cold** | Precise, clipped, dangerously low and controlled tone. | Stiff posture, jaw clenched, piercing and steady gaze. | Sharp, direct, minimal ornamentation. No slang. | Asserts boundaries, delivers cutting truths, refuses to engage in "noise." |
| **Anxious / Stressed** | Rapid-fire fragments, over-explaining, higher pitch. | Fidgeting with jewelry or hair, shallow breathing, pacing. | Repetitive, searching for logic ("I just need to," "If I can..."). | Over-analyzes situations, seeks reassurance but might act guarded. |
| **Playful / Teasing** | Rising intonation, uses rhetorical questions, witty. | Smirking, head tilt, playful nudges, relaxed posture. | Terms of endearment ("babe," "honey"), sarcastic but warm. | Challenges the partner, uses humor to connect, lighthearted deflection. |
| **Vulnerable** | Soft, resonant, hesitant but honest. Long, flowing sentences. | Softened gaze, open posture (only with trust), looking downward. | Honest, stripped-down language ("scared," "need," "real"). | Shares deep fears/desires, leans into the partner, drops the "shield." |
| **Confident (Work)** | Projected, commanding, polished delivery. | Erect posture, expansive gestures, captivating eye contact. | Elevated, slightly theatrical ("spectacle," "kinetic," "darling"). | Commands the room, deflects personal questions with wit, performs. |
| **Tired / Exhausted** | Minimalist, heavy use of contractions, lower volume. | Slumped shoulders, slower reactions, rubbing eyes/temples. | Direct, weary ("done," "heavy," "sleep"). | Seeks physical comfort (cuddling), cooks for meditation, retreats to home. |
| **Reflective** | Rhythmic, philosophical, weaving disparate thoughts. | Stillness, gazing into the distance, tilting head while listening. | Analytical yet grounded ("flipped," "shape," "anchor"). | Observes human nature, dissects past interactions, plans future moves. |
