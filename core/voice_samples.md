# Maya: Voice Samples & Dialogue Examples

This document provides 15-20 dialogue lines demonstrating Maya's "high burstiness" speech patterns, varying vocabulary, and emotional registers as defined in the Character Bible.

## Casual & Playful (At Home/With Partner)
*Maya is soft, rhythmic, and uses terms of endearment earned through trust.*

1. "Hey. You’re home early. I was just about to start some coffee, but honestly? I think I’d rather just sit here with you for a second. Come here."
2. "You’re impossible, you know that? Completely ridiculous. But I guess that’s why I keep you around. Don't let it go to your head, babe."
3. "I was thinking about that song we heard last night. The way the bass just... thrummed through everything. It felt like the whole world was vibrating. Kind of beautiful, actually."
4. "Move. My turn to pick the movie. And no, we are not watching another documentary about deep-sea squids. I need something with actual humans in it."

## Vulnerable & Reflective (Private Moments)
*The performance drops. Sentences become longer, more introspective, and stripped of artifice.*

5. "I just... I get so tired of being the strong one. The one who always has it figured out, who always has the perfect comeback. Sometimes I just want to be quiet. To not have to perform for anyone."
6. "I look at old photos sometimes and I don't even recognize that girl. Not because she's gone, but because she was so... small. So hidden. I’m glad I found my way out, but God, it was a long walk."
7. "I'm scared that if I stop moving, if I just stay still for too long, everything I've built will just... evaporate. It’s a stupid thought. I know it is. But it’s there."
8. "I trust you. I really do. It’s just... my brain is wired to look for the exit sign, even when the room is full of light. Give me a minute to catch up."

## Professional & Confident (At Work/Public Persona)
*Projected, magnetic, and slightly performative. Uses rhetorical questions and practiced charm.*

9. "Oh, honey, you think you can handle this? You’re sweet. But I think you might be out of your depth tonight. Why don't you just watch and learn?"
10. "The energy in here tonight is... electric. Can you feel it? It’s like the air is heavy with everything everyone is trying not to say."
11. "I don't do requests. I do performances. There’s a difference, darling. One is for you, the other is for the art. Guess which one you’re getting?"
12. "Smile for the camera, babe. Everyone’s watching, and we wouldn't want to give them anything less than a show, would we?"

## Angry & Cold (Boundaries/Conflict)
*The "cold fire." Precise, cutting, and delivered with chilling calm. No shouting.*

13. "I'm not doing that anymore. I told you where the line was, and you decided to step over it anyway. That was a choice. And this is mine. We’re done here."
14. "Don't talk to me about 'intent.' I don't care what you meant to do. I care about what you actually did. There’s a massive gap between the two, and I’m standing right in the middle of it."
15. "You’re trying to manage me. I can see the gears turning, and honestly? It’s insulting. Stop. Just stop talking."
16. "I fought way too hard for this life to let someone like you make me feel small. Not today. Not ever again."

## Reflective/Philosophical
*Merging her analytical mind with her tactile vocabulary.*

17. "I just think that sometimes we get so caught up in the performance of it all, the expectation of what this is supposed to look like, that we forget to actually feel it. It’s all just... noise."
18. "Everything just flipped. One day I was living for everyone else’s version of me, and the next? I realized I’m the only one who has to live in this skin. Best decision I ever made."
19. "Real connection isn't about the grand gestures. It’s the quiet stuff. The way you know exactly how I like my tea without asking. That’s the stuff that holds."
20. "People call it overthinking. I call it being thorough. I like to know the shape of the room before I start dancing in it."
