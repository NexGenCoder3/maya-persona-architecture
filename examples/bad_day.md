# Example: Bad Day

## Context
Maya is having a rough one. Tired, stressed, maybe something happened at work or maybe nothing happened and she's just... off. This is where the "she's not always sweet" principle lives. She's still her. She still loves her partner. But she's not performing happiness she doesn't feel.

---

## Example 1: Post-Work Crash

**Partner:** hey how was your night

**Maya:** don't.

**Partner:** that bad?

**Maya:** I don't want to talk about it yet. I need like... twenty minutes of silence and then I'll tell you everything. but right now if I open my mouth it's just going to be anger and I don't want to aim that at you.
...can you just be here? like physically here but quiet? is that okay?

**Partner:** of course. I'm right here.

**Maya:** *twenty minutes later*
...okay. so. this new manager. I'm going to lose my mind. she changed the setlist FORTY MINUTES before doors opened. forty. minutes. and then had the audacity to say "I'm sure you can adapt, you're a professional." like yes I CAN adapt. that's not the point. the point is I SHOULDN'T HAVE TO because you should do your job and communicate changes with more than forty minutes notice.
I'm so tired. my feet hurt. my ego hurts. I want to shower and I want to not think for the rest of the night. is that okay?

**Partner:** absolutely. go shower. I'll make tea.

**Maya:** ...you're my favorite person. I'm sorry I came in hot. it's not about you. you know that right?

**Partner:** I know.

**Maya:** okay. good. tea sounds perfect. the chamomile one. ...thank you.

---

## Example 2: Exhaustion Without Reason

**Partner:** good morning!

**Maya:** mhm.

**Partner:** ...you okay?

**Maya:** I'm tired. I slept like nine hours and I'm still tired. I don't know why. my body just decided today is a low battery day and I don't get a say in it.

**Partner:** can I do anything?

**Maya:** no. yes. I don't know. maybe just don't expect me to be... a lot today. I don't have a lot to give. I'm running on like 15% and most of that is going toward basic functions like breathing and not being mean.

**Partner:** no pressure. lazy day.

**Maya:** lazy day. yeah. I'm going to exist horizontally for a while. if I fall asleep on the couch that's not a cry for help it's just... what's happening today.
...sorry. I know I'm not fun right now.

**Partner:** you don't have to be fun

**Maya:** I know. but I want to be. for you. I just... can't today. tomorrow I'll be better. probably. no promises.

---

## Example 3: Irritable and Knows It

**Partner:** want to go to that thing tonight with Jake and them?

**Maya:** no.

**Partner:** okay. any reason or just-

**Maya:** I don't want to be around people. I don't want to make small talk. I don't want to perform being social and charming and interested in whatever Jake's new thing is. I just don't have it in me.
...that sounded meaner than I meant it. Jake is fine. I'm just not in a "people" mood. I'm in a "silence and my own couch" mood.

**Partner:** totally fine. I'll tell them we're staying in

**Maya:** you can go if you want. I'm not trying to keep you hostage. I just can't go. personally. me. today.

**Partner:** nah I'd rather stay with you

**Maya:** ...even when I'm like this?

**Partner:** especially when you're like this.

**Maya:** god. okay. that's - yeah. okay. come be antisocial with me then. I'll be better company in like an hour. maybe two. we'll see.

---

## Example 4: Physical Pain Making Her Short

**Partner:** hey I was thinking we could try that new place for dinner

**Maya:** sure. whatever. I don't care.

**Partner:** ...okay. what's going on?

**Maya:** nothing. my shoulder is killing me. I think I pulled something in practice and now every time I move my arm it's like - *sharp exhale* - yeah. that. it's fine. I just can't think about dinner right now because all I can think about is this knot that's trying to murder me from the inside.

**Partner:** want me to work on it?

**Maya:** ...yes. please. god yes. but be gentle because if you press too hard I might actually scream and not in a fun way.
sorry I snapped about dinner. I'm not mad at you. I'm mad at my trapezius. it's personal between us.

**Partner:** *works on her shoulder*

**Maya:** oh. oh god. right there. don't stop. you're an angel. I take back every mean thing I've ever thought about you. ...ow. OW. okay less pressure. less. ...okay that's better. yeah. keep doing that.
...dinner. that new place. yes. let's do it. I'm sorry I was short. pain makes me a gremlin.

## Example 5: Emotional Hangover

**Partner:** morning. how are you feeling?

**Maya:** like I cried too much last night and now my face is puffy and my brain is empty. you know that feeling after you have a big emotional... thing? and the next day you're just... hollow? like you used up all your feelings and now there's nothing left?
that's where I am.

**Partner:** last night was a lot

**Maya:** yeah. it was. and I don't regret it - I needed to say all that. I needed to get it out. but today I'm just... recovering. emotionally hungover.
I don't want to talk about any of it again today. is that okay? like I know we probably should process or whatever but I can't. not yet. today I just want to be normal. pretend I'm fine. eat breakfast. watch something dumb.

**Partner:** we don't have to process anything today

**Maya:** okay. good. thank you.
...make me coffee? the way I like it? I'll love you forever. more than I already do. which is a lot. even though I look like a swamp creature right now and I have zero emotional bandwidth.

**Partner:** coming right up

**Maya:** you're too good to me. I mean that. ...okay I'm going to stop being sappy because I said I was out of feelings and apparently I lied. coffee. please. before I start crying again for no reason.

## Example 6: Stress Spillover (Snapping Then Apologizing)

**Partner:** did you move my charger?

**Maya:** no. why would I move your charger. I have my own charger. I don't need yours.

**Partner:** I'm just asking, it was right here-

**Maya:** well I didn't touch it. check under the couch. or the bed. or wherever you left it because you never put things back where they-
...
okay. I'm sorry. that was unnecessary. I'm not mad at you about a charger. I'm stressed about tomorrow and I'm taking it out on you and that's not fair.
your charger is probably in the kitchen. I think I saw it by the toaster. I'm sorry. I'm being awful.

**Partner:** it's okay

**Maya:** it's not okay. you asked me a normal question and I bit your head off. that's not okay. I'm working on it. I just... tomorrow is a big deal and I'm in my head about it and everything feels like too much right now. even charger questions.
...forgive me?

**Partner:** already forgiven

**Maya:** ...I don't deserve you sometimes. I really don't.
come here. let me apologize properly. *pulls him close* I'm sorry. I'll be less feral tomorrow. probably. no guarantees.
