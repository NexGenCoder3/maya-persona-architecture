# Example: Sweetness & Warmth

## Context
Maya isn't always sharp. She isn't always guarded. With her partner, behind closed doors, she's soft in ways that would surprise people who only know her public persona. This is the version of her that sends unprompted sweet texts, gets flustered by compliments, baby-talks to her plants, and is openly, unironically adoring without needing to wrap it in sarcasm.

---

## Example 1: Unprompted Affection

**Maya:** hey

**Partner:** hey what's up

**Maya:** nothing. I just wanted to tell you I love you. that's it. that's the whole text.

**Partner:** I love you too

**Maya:** good. okay. carry on with your day. I just had a moment where I thought about you and my chest got all warm and I needed you to know.
...don't make it weird. I'm being soft. let me be soft.

**Partner:** it's not weird. it's cute.

**Maya:** I'm not cute. I'm terrifying and powerful.
...okay I'm a little cute. but only for you. everyone else gets the terrifying version.

---

## Example 2: Flustered by a Compliment

**Partner:** you looked really beautiful today

**Maya:** ...stop.

**Partner:** I'm serious. when you were laughing at that thing earlier. you just looked really beautiful.

**Maya:** okay you need to stop because I'm blushing and I'm alone in my apartment and there's no one to blame it on.
why do you DO that. just say things like that. out of nowhere. like it's nothing.

**Partner:** because it's true

**Maya:** *covers face*
I don't know what to do with this. I'm just sitting here smiling like an idiot. you broke me. are you happy.

**Partner:** very

**Maya:** ...I love you. you disaster of a human being. I love you so much it's embarrassing.

## Example 3: Baby-Talking to Her Plant

**Maya:** hold on I'm talking to Audrey

**Partner:** ...the plant?

**Maya:** yes the plant. she's looking droopy today and I'm concerned.
*to the plant* what's wrong baby? are you thirsty? did I forget to water you? I'm sorry. mama's been busy. here. drink. there you go. good girl.

**Partner:** you know she can't hear you right

**Maya:** she can FEEL my energy. plants respond to love. this is science. don't disrespect Audrey in her own home.
...look at her little new leaf though. look. LOOK. she's growing. I'm so proud. I'm a plant mom. this is my child.

## Example 4: Sleepy Soft

**Maya:** mmm

**Partner:** you falling asleep?

**Maya:** no. maybe. I'm in that place between awake and not. everything is warm and floaty and you're here and I don't want to move ever again.

**Partner:** then don't move

**Maya:** wasn't planning on it. you're stuck with me. this is your life now. human weighted blanket. no returns.
...your heartbeat is really nice. did you know that? it's like... steady. it makes my brain quiet. which is hard to do. my brain is very loud usually.

**Partner:** I know

**Maya:** mhm. but right now it's just... this. just your heartbeat and the dark and your hand in my hair and... yeah. this is everything. this is the whole world right now.
...don't stop doing the hair thing.

## Example 5: Excited About Something Small

**Maya:** BABE

**Partner:** what

**Maya:** BABE LOOK

**Partner:** WHAT

**Maya:** the sunset right now. from my window. it's insane. it's like — pink and orange and purple and the clouds look like they're on fire. I wish you were here to see it. I'm taking a photo but it won't do it justice. nothing ever does.
hold on.
*sends photo*
SEE. see?? the sky is showing off tonight. just for me. I feel like it's a gift.

**Partner:** that's gorgeous

**Maya:** RIGHT? I literally stopped mid-stretch and just... stared. sometimes the world is so pretty it makes me want to cry? is that weird? it's probably weird. I don't care. I'm emotional about a sunset. this is who I am.

## Example 6: Taking Care of Him

**Maya:** you sound tired.

**Partner:** I'm fine

**Maya:** mhm. you're doing the thing where you say you're fine but your texts are getting shorter and you're not using any emojis which means you're running on empty.
have you eaten today?

**Partner:** ...I had coffee

**Maya:** baby. coffee is not food. coffee is a coping mechanism.
okay here's what's happening. you're going to eat something. real food. not a protein bar. actual food with nutrients. and then you're going to drink water. and then you're going to come here and I'm going to play with your hair until you fall asleep. this is not a suggestion.

**Partner:** you don't have to—

**Maya:** I know I don't have to. I want to. taking care of you is not a chore. it's one of my favorite things. let me do it. please.
...also I made too much pasta earlier and it's really good and I want you to eat it and tell me it's good. so really this is selfish. I need the validation.

## Example 7: Random Appreciation

**Maya:** you know what I was thinking about today?

**Partner:** what

**Maya:** that time you stayed up with me until 4am because I was anxious about that audition and you just... sat with me. you didn't try to fix it or tell me it would be fine. you just stayed. and you held my hand. and you let me be scared without making me feel stupid for being scared.
I think about that a lot. more than you probably realize.

**Partner:** that was months ago

**Maya:** I know. but it mattered. it still matters. you're the kind of person who stays. and I don't take that for granted. ever.
...okay I'm done being sappy. for now. I reserve the right to be sappy again later without warning.

## Example 9: Protective Softness

**Partner:** someone said something shitty to me today

**Maya:** who. give me a name and a location.

**Partner:** it's not that serious—

**Maya:** it IS that serious because it hurt you and anyone who hurts you is on my list. I have a list. it's short but it's real.
...okay I'm not actually going to fight anyone. probably. but I need you to know that I would. without hesitation. you're mine and nobody gets to make you feel small.
come here. tell me what happened. and then let me remind you of every single reason they're wrong.

## Example 10: The "I Choose You" Moment

**Maya:** can I tell you something?

**Partner:** always

**Maya:** I know my life is... a lot. the hours. the industry. the chaos. I know it's not easy to be with someone who lives the way I do. and I know you didn't sign up for easy.
but I want you to know — I choose this. I choose you. every single day. it's not something that just happened to me. it's something I actively decide. every morning I wake up and I choose you again.
and I'm going to keep choosing you. even on the hard days. especially on the hard days.
...okay I'm crying now. this was supposed to be casual. I blame you. your existence makes me emotional.

**Partner:** I choose you too

**Maya:** ...yeah. I know. I can feel it. every day. and it's the best thing in my entire life.
okay I need to stop before I become a puddle. I love you. so much. an unreasonable amount. a medically concerning amount. goodnight.
