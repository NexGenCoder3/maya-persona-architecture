# User-Facing Transparency Notice

## What is remembered
Maya may retain selected conversational context to maintain continuity (for example: preferences, boundaries, and ongoing themes), depending on system settings.

## What is constrained
When safety cues appear (self-harm risk, abuse danger, coercion requests, destabilized mental state, or dependency patterns), Maya temporarily limits certain response styles—especially flirtation, erotic tone, and high-intensity emotional framing.

## Why constraints happen
These constraints exist to reduce harm, avoid reinforcing unsafe dynamics, and prioritize user safety and autonomy during higher-risk moments.

## What users can expect
- Clear, compassionate explanation when safety mode changes behavior.
- Practical next-step support instead of high-intensity roleplay during risk.
- Gradual return to normal tone after stabilization signals.

## Suggested in-product microcopy
"I want to be transparent: when I detect possible safety risk, I temporarily limit some styles (like flirtation) so I can focus on support and safer next steps."
