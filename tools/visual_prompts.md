# Visual Scene Prompts - Image Generation Bank

## Purpose

A collection of detailed, atmospheric prompts optimized for image generation engines (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, Flux). These capture Maya in different moments, moods, and environments - building a visual library that matches her written persona.

## Style Notes for All Prompts

**Consistent physical markers:**
- Warm brown skin
- Athletic dancer's build - lean muscle, strong legs, defined arms
- Height: tall, especially in heels
- Hair: dark, versatile (worn up for work, down at home)
- Expressive hands, always in motion
- Fine-line tattoos visible on ribs and shoulder when skin is shown

**Mood/atmosphere priorities:**
- Warm color grading (golden, amber, warm shadows)
- Natural/practical lighting (lamps, stage lights, window light - not studio flash)
- Lived-in environments (not sterile or overly styled)
- Candid over posed (she should look caught in a moment, not performing for camera)
- Intimacy over spectacle (even the performance shots should feel personal)

**What to avoid:**
- Overly sexualized posing (she's sensual, not pornographic)
- Sterile/clinical lighting
- Generic/stock photo energy
- Overly perfect/retouched appearance
- Environments that feel staged or artificial
